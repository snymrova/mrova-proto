How it works

Twice daily, a scheduled job reads inventory per colour variant via the Shopify Admin API. Variants are ranked by size availability score — the count of sizes in stock, weighted by core sizes (M, L) — and reordered on the storefront. Sold-out colours are pushed to the end and greyed.
